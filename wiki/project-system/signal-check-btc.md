# Signal Check BTC

x 5/15/30/45 min. Ridge and an MLP predict those 8 results from the 205-feature photo (trained fev–mai). The bot takes
the best predicted entry if it beats the threshold, one position at a time, funding charged (`treino.opera`). June
is out of sample; the frozen test (07/jul–out) is not touched. "Never trade" = 0%.

## Reading (2026-11-06)

- MLP memorizes fev–mai (+2000% in sample) and loses in June (-44%): too much capacity for this signal.
- Ridge: its 1% most confident minutes average about +0.12% to +0.13% per episode in both periods (fev–mai 151 episodes, 79 positive; June 38 episodes, 22 positive). It is 98% long in June while BTC fell 20.5% and "always long 45 min" lost -0.144% per entry, so it is not trend-following. Heaviest weights: legacy smart-money delta (+), total delta (-), volume ratio (-): buy when large traders buy into aggregate selling.
- Weak and rare: 15 trades in June at the 0.10% threshold. Not proven; the frozen test decides.
- NCM run 1 (GRPO): tokenizer 100%, but the policy stopped trading by step 200 of 8000 (all K samples flat -> zero advantage -> no gradient). Method failure, not evidence.
- Versions seen in June so far: 170 (NCM run 1) + 10 (this page).
